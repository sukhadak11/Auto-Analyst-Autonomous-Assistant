KEY OBSERVED RELATIONSHIPS WITH CHURN

1. Customer service calls
   - Customers who churned averaged 2.23 customer service calls, versus 1.45 for customers who stayed.
   - Customers with 4 or more service calls churned at a rate of 51.69% (267 customers in this group).
   - Customers with fewer than 4 service calls churned at a rate of 11.25% (3,066 customers in this group).
   - This is the single largest observed gap in the dataset between churned and retained customers.

2. International plan
   - Customers with an international plan churned at 42.41%.
   - Customers without an international plan churned at 11.50%.
   - Having an international plan is associated with substantially higher churn in this dataset.

3. Voice mail plan
   - Customers with a voice mail plan churned at 8.68%.
   - Customers without a voice mail plan churned at 16.72%.
   - Having a voice mail plan is associated with lower churn in this dataset.

4. Daytime usage (day_mins)
   - Churned customers averaged 206.9 daytime minutes, versus 175.2 minutes for retained customers.
   - Higher daytime usage is associated with higher churn in this dataset.

5. Total charges
   - Churned customers averaged $65.36 in total charges, versus $58.45 for retained customers.
   - Higher total billed amount is associated with higher churn in this dataset.

6. Account length (tenure)
   - Churned customers averaged an account length of 102.7, versus 100.8 for retained customers.
   - Account length alone shows only a small difference between churned and retained customers in this dataset.
